Frequently Asked Questions

Need help with something else? Send us an enquiry.

How often should solar panels be cleaned?

Cleaning frequency depends on local dust, weather, nearby trees or construction, bird activity and the panel layout. Contact us for a recommendation based on your site.

Does dust affect solar panel performance?

Dust and other buildup can cover part of a panel surface. Keeping the surface clean is a sensible part of routine solar care.

Is solar panel cleaning safe?

It should be performed with appropriate equipment, methods and site-specific safety procedures. We assess accessible areas and the cleaning requirement before service.

Can you clean residential rooftop solar panels?

Yes. Residential rooftop solar cleaning is one of our core services. Share your location and system details to request a quote.

Do you provide commercial solar cleaning?

Yes. We support offices, shops, hotels and commercial buildings, with plans that can be tailored to the site.

Do you offer recurring maintenance plans?

Yes. Scheduled cleaning and maintenance plans are available for customers who prefer ongoing solar care.

Do you provide AMC services?

Yes. Our AMC options can include scheduled cleaning, visual inspection and service reminders. Ask for a plan matched to your installation.

How do I book a service?

You can use the form below, call us, or message us on WhatsApp. We will collect a few details about your system and requirements.

How much does solar panel cleaning cost?

Cost depends on system size, access, property type, condition and service scope. Request a free quote for accurate pricing.

Do you provide free quotations?

Yes. Tell us about your installation and preferred service, and we will help you with the appropriate next step.
